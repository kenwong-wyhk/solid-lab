Angle between two planes
HKDSE F5 · 3D Trigonometry

The angle between two planes is the angle between two lines — one in each plane — both perpendicular to the line of intersection at the same point. （兩平面的夾角）
  1. 1Place P on the intersection line.
  2. 2Draw a perpendicular in the first plane.
  3. 3Draw the matching perpendicular in the second plane.
